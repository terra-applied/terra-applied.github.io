Scaled Simulation, Minimal Demonstration

Terra series research created by Applied Intuition aims at pushing the boundary of physical AI learned in closed loop with minimal human demonstration. Terra series exploits high-throughput, reactive simulation at scale together with efficient closed-loop reinforcement learning to bring the generalizability and robustness of physical AI to the next level.
